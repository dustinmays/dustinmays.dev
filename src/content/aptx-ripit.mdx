A strength training app for older adults and gym newcomers, in limited release
at Ironworks gym in Boulder. Built for people (like my mom) who feel
intimidated by gyms. Quirky retro design with a frog mascot, on purpose: the
gym should feel less like a tech product and more like a place. Stack is
Next.js, Go, Postgres, Redis, K8s. I run it on a self-hosted cluster.
