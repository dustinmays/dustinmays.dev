Before Aptx Health, I led quality engineering at Freewave Technologies on
IoT/embedded systems. I went from junior to lead in three years and ran a
team of six. The work I'm proudest of: finding a vendor lock-in risk no one
else had flagged, advocating for an alternative, building it, and saving the
company roughly eighty thousand dollars a year. Also: replacing a thirty-to-sixty
minute manual provisioning process (with a ten-percent error rate) with an
automated pipeline.

Before engineering, I was an occupational therapist. Brain injury rehab,
senior living, custom assistive technology. I built things for individual
patients with PVC, fabric, and a 3D printer, then worked with their families
and care teams to fit the work into their lives. At VCU, two classmates and I
built a swiveling computer station for a man with quadriplegia. He later used
it to <a
href="https://news.vcu.edu/article/They_created_a_computer_station__and_changed_a_quadriplegic_patients"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">call for help during a medical emergency</a>.
